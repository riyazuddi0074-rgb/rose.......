About rose

The rose is often called the "Queen of Flowers" and is widely loved for its stunning beauty and delightful fragrance. Belonging to the Rosa genus, roses come in thousands of varieties and a vast spectrum of colors, including red, pink, yellow, white, and orange, each carrying its own symbolic meaning. Beyond their decorative charm, roses play a significant role in various industries—their essential oils and petals are used to create perfumes, cosmetics, rose water, and even herbal teas. Native to regions across Asia, Europe, North America, and northwestern Africa, these woody perennial plants feature stems adorned with sharp thorns that serve as a natural defense. Whether grown in home gardens or gifted on special occasions, the rose remains an enduring global symbol of love, elegance, and appreciation.
